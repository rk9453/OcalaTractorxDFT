What we found on ocalatractor.com

Great inventory. The doors close at 5.

We went through your site, inventory and listings the way a buyer would. The equipment and pricing are strong. The gaps are all in what happens after someone raises a hand.

Every request waits for a callback

Schedule Demo, Service Appointments, Parts Request and Value Your Trade are all forms. Nothing books a real time, so the buyer waits until someone is back at a desk.

No one answers after hours

Your Ocala store lists 8am to 5pm, Monday to Friday. Hobby farmers and weekend property owners shop evenings and Saturdays, and there's no chat or text option on the site.

Three stores, one front door

Ocala, Palatka and Eustis each have their own sales and finance people, but online leads arrive in one place. Palatka buyers shouldn't wait on the Ocala inbox.

Package deals with no next step

"The Weekend Rancher," "Honey, I Dug a Hole," "The Money Maker": your packages are some of the best-named in Florida. None of them have a "book a demo ride" button.

Rentals that never become sales

You rent the TYM T130 from $650 a day. A customer who rents twice is a buyer, but nothing flags them or makes the offer.

Search details are out of date

Your page description still lists Rhino AG and Bobcat, and the Eustis map link points to "Lake City." Small, but it's what Google reads first.
